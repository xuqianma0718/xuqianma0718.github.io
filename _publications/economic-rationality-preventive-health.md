<p class="research-eyebrow">Job Market Paper</p>

<p class="page__lead">Preventive health decisions often require people to weigh an immediate cost against an uncertain future benefit. This paper studies whether individuals whose choices more consistently satisfy revealed-preference conditions are more likely to invest in preventive health.</p>

The project brings measures of economic rationality into empirical work on health behavior. It asks whether differences in the consistency of individual choice help explain preventive-health investments and what those differences imply for the design of health policy.
